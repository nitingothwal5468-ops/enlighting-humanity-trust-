Food for Hunger (Project Annapurna)

Ensuring nutritional security and dignity for daily-wage migrant laborers, children, and elderly residents in outer Delhi.

Program Overview

Fighting Urban Hunger at the Grassroots Level

In the industrial fringes and construction settlements of Tikri Kalan, Neelwal, and Mundka, hundreds of daily-wage laborers face intermittent work. When a day's wage is lost, the evening stove remains unlit.

Through Project Annapurna, Enlighting Humanity Trust steps in to provide continuous food relief. We organize freshly cooked, hygienic meal distribution drives and distribute monthly dry ration hampers containing staples like wheat flour (atta), pulses (dal), rice, cooking mustard oil, and iodized salt.

Our focus is not merely calories, but balanced nutrition. Malnutrition in early childhood causes irreversible cognitive and physical stunting. We supply special fruit, milk, and protein supplement kits directly to preschool and primary-age children.

🍲

Hot Nutritious Meals

Cooked with pure mustard oil and wholesome grains, served hot to migrant workers, cycle rickshaw pullers, and street dwellers.

📦

Monthly Dry Ration Kits

Each family ration box contains 10kg Atta, 5kg Rice, 2kg Pulses, 1L Cooking Oil, Salt, and essential spices to support a family of 4 for a month.

🥛

Child Nutrition Supplement

Fortified milk powder, jaggery (gur), roasted chana, and seasonal fruits provided weekly to underprivileged children in our study pods.
