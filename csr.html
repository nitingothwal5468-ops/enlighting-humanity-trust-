Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Impactful, compliant CSR solutions aligned with Section 135 and Schedule VII of the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

Statutory Corporate Alignment

Measurable, Audited CSR Implementation in Delhi-NCR

For corporate partners seeking genuine on-the-ground execution, Enlighting Humanity Trust provides end-to-end CSR implementation backed by strict statutory compliance, photographic reporting, and audited utilization certificates.

All our initiatives are mapped directly to Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013. We work with corporate CSR committees to structure clear Memorandums of Understanding (MoU), defined KPIs, baseline surveys, and periodic milestone audits.

Eligible CSR Focus Areas

Partner with us across core areas approved under Section 135 of the Companies Act.

Clause (i)

Eradicating Hunger & Malnutrition

Hot meals and monthly dry ration distributions for migrant laborers and nutritional packs for children via Project Annapurna.

Clause (ii)

Promoting Education

Remedial learning pods, school starter kits, digital literacy labs, and dropout re-enrollment campaigns.

Clause (iii)

Empowering Women & Gender Equality

Vocational sewing training, micro-enterprise support, and adolescent menstrual hygiene initiatives.

Clause (iv)

Environmental Sustainability

Monthly native tree plantation drives (20+ trees/mo), agroforestry, and environmental education with children.

Clause (ii)

Vocational Skills & Livelihood

Computer literacy, basic IT software, spoken English, and job-readiness for underprivileged youth.

Clause (x)

Rural Community Development

Apna Aangan flagship community center infrastructure, clean drinking water, and village health camps.
