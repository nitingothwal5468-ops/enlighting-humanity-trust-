Privacy Policy

How Enlighting Humanity Trust safeguards your personal information and respects your data rights.

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

Enlighting Humanity Trust ("we," "our," or "the Trust") respects the privacy of all visitors, donors, volunteers, and partners who interact with our website and programs. This Privacy Policy details our practices in compliance with the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information when voluntarily submitted by you:

  • Donor Information: Name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, PAN (for 80G tax receipt issuance), transaction reference number (UTR), and postal address.
  • Volunteer & Partner Inquiries: Name, educational background, professional skills, contact numbers, and location.
  • Technical Information: Standard server logs, browser type, and anonymous aggregate visit counts. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies.

3. Purpose of Data Use

The information collected is strictly utilized to:

  • Issue official 80G tax deduction receipts and donation confirmations.
  • Communicate project progress reports and quarterly community impact updates.
  • Coordinate volunteer activities, field visits, and emergency relief deployments.
  • Comply with statutory reporting requirements under the Indian Trusts Act and Income Tax Department.

4. Absolute Non-Disclosure Policy

We never sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal or financial data to any commercial third party, marketing agency, or external entity. Your information is strictly retained within the administrative custody of Enlighting Humanity Trust.

5. Financial Data Security

Direct UPI payments and QR code transactions are processed directly through your respective banking or UPI application (e.g., Paytm, BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe). Enlighting Humanity Trust never stores, sees, or accesses your bank passwords, debit card numbers, or UPI PINs.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to erroneous details, or request the removal of your email from our newsletter updates. For any privacy requests, please contact our Data Redressal Officer at:
