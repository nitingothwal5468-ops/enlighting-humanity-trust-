Volunteer With Us

Lend your time, skills, and heart to create measurable social impact in Village Neelwal and Tikri Kalan.

Be The Change

Join A Vibrant Community of Grassroots Changemakers

Volunteering with Enlighting Humanity Trust is not passive charity. You will work on the ground alongside village children, daily-wage families, and local youth, gaining authentic leadership experience.

Whether you can dedicate two hours every Sunday to teach English or math, participate in our monthly tree-planting drives, or offer your graphic design and digital skills remotely, your contribution matters immensely.

✓ Certificate of Volunteering: Official certificate recognizing your grassroots hours.
✓ Flexible Modes: On-field in West Delhi or remote digital volunteering.
✓ Direct Ground Exposure: Experience real social development firsthand.

Available Volunteer Roles

📚

Weekend Tutor / Mentor

Teach basic English, Mathematics, science experiments, or arts and crafts to primary school children in Village Neelwal.

🌱

Tree Plantation Coordinator

Help organize our monthly 20-tree plantation drives: sourcing native saplings, coordinating with schools, and placing tree guards.

🎨

Digital, Design & Media

Design posters, edit field videos, manage social media stories, and help document ground activities remotely.
