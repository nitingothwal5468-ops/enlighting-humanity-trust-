About Our Foundation

Rooted in Grassroots Empathy, Driven by Measurable Change

"True empowerment does not come from charity from afar, but from standing shoulder to shoulder with the most marginalized families in our villages and resettlement clusters."

Established under the Indian Trusts Act in West Delhi, Enlighting Humanity Trust works directly at ground level in Village Neelwal, Post Office Tikri Kalan, and surrounding semi-urban clusters. Founded by Nitin Gothwal, our organization is committed to transparent social service where every rupee, hour, and resource directly reaches those who need it most.

Signature Environmental Pledge

20 Native Trees Every Month, Planted with Local Children

Rather than ceremonial one-day plantation stunts, Enlighting Humanity Trust runs a continuous, disciplined ecological drive. Every month, rain or shine, we gather village children, teachers, and volunteers to plant at least 20 native trees (Neem, Peepal, Jamun, Shisham) across Village Neelwal, Tikri Kalan, and rural schools.

240+
Native trees planted each year

Protected with durable tree guards and adopted by student "Tree Buddies" for 100% survival rate.

  • Native drought-resistant species suited to Delhi's soil.
  • Geotagged monitoring and regular watering schedules.
  • Instilling lifetime environmental empathy in next-generation leaders.
