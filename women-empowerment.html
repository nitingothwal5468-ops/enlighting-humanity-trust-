Women Empowerment & Self-Reliance

Fostering financial independence, vocational stitching skills, and menstrual hygiene awareness in rural Delhi.

Dignity & Livelihood

Unlocking Economic Independence for Rural Women

In semi-rural communities like Village Neelwal and Tikri Kalan, many women spend their lives confined to unpaid domestic labor without any personal savings or financial decision-making power.

Enlighting Humanity Trust provides free tailoring, sewing, and embroidery classes equipped with durable sewing machines. Women learn cutting, garment assembly, and traditional needlework, enabling them to accept stitching orders from local families and markets.

Beyond livelihoods, we address the critical issue of menstrual health. Period poverty remains pervasive; we conduct regular health workshops and distribute free eco-friendly sanitary napkins to adolescent girls and mothers.

🧵

Tailoring & Sewing Center

6-month structured training covering garment construction, alteration, hand embroidery, and machine maintenance.

🩸

Menstrual Dignity Drives

Educating adolescent girls on reproductive health, debunking myths, and distributing free sanitary pad packages.

💰

Self-Help Group (SHG) Savings

Encouraging women to form pooled micro-saving circles, opening personal bank accounts, and building emergency funds.
